Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Version française : lire ce document en français.

MarV Route is made by MarV Space, an assumed business name registered in the State of Idaho, USA, by an independent developer based in Boise, Idaho ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the MarV Route mobile app and the marvroute.com website collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

📍 Background location notice

MarV Route collects location data to record your trips, and can do so in the background — including when the app is closed or not in use — but only while you have trip tracking or automatic trip detection switched on. Your phone shows when this is happening: a persistent notification on Android, the location indicator on iPhone. This data is used solely to measure and save your own trips inside the app. It is never sold, and it is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can turn tracking off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

1. Information We Collect

What we collect depends on how you use MarV Route:

📍 Location Data

  • Precise GPS location while a trip is being recorded and while automatic trip detection is watching for a trip to start — including in the background.
  • We use it to measure distance and to record each trip's route, start and end points, date and time.
  • Detailed GPS route traces stay on your device. They are never uploaded to the cloud; they leave your phone only inside a backup file that you export yourself.

🚗 Trips, Expenses and Settings

  • Whether a trip is business or personal, its platform and purpose, and any edits you make.
  • Fuel fill-ups, parking, tolls, repairs and other expenses you log, and your maintenance reminders.
  • Your preferences: distance unit, country and currency, language, theme and Work Mode settings.

🪪 Profile, Vehicle and Report Details (optional)

  • Details you choose to enter so they can appear on your reports: your name and your business name.
  • Your vehicle: its name, make, model, year, licence plate and odometer readings, and the oil-change and brake-pad service data you enter for maintenance reminders.
  • Your insurance: the company's name and contact details, and your policy name and number.
  • Your accountant's name, phone number and address.
  • Every one of these fields is optional. If you enter details about another person, such as your accountant, make sure you are allowed to share them.

👤 Account Information (optional)

  • You can use MarV Route without an account. In that case we don't receive your email address, and nothing is stored in the cloud.
  • If you create an account with an email and password: your email address and the user ID that Firebase Authentication assigns to you. Your password is checked by Firebase Authentication — we never see it.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Firebase Authentication.
  • To keep you signed in, the app may keep your sign-in details in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). They are not sent anywhere from there and are removed when you sign out.

🌐 Website and Launch List

  • If you ask to be notified about the launch on marvroute.com, we collect the name and email address you enter, the driver type you select, the language you viewed the site in, and the time of your request.
  • We use them only to send you the launch announcement — a single email. It will show our postal address and include a way to unsubscribe, and you can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us.
  • The website sets no cookies and has no advertising or analytics trackers. It only remembers your language and theme in your own browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when you open a page and may keep it in its security logs.

🛠 Diagnostics and Statistics

  • If you allow it in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may share crash reports and basic diagnostics with us, such as the device model, OS version and where the app crashed.
  • Apple (App Store Connect) and Google (Google Play Console) also give us aggregated statistics about installs, usage and crashes. These statistics do not identify you individually.
  • We use this information only to fix bugs and improve the app. MarV Route itself does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Record trips and generate reports
  • Add up your mileage and, for drivers in the United States, estimate the standard mileage deduction for your records
  • Back up and restore your data while you are signed in and cloud backup is active
  • Send the notifications you allow, such as trip alerts and maintenance reminders
  • Answer your support requests, fix bugs and improve the app
  • Send the launch announcement, if you asked for it on our website

We do not use your location or trip data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Where your data lives depends on how you use the app:

  • On your device — your trips, expenses and settings are always saved on your phone. If you use the app without an account, that is the only place they are kept.
  • In the cloud, while you are signed in and cloud backup is active — cloud backup is a Pro feature. While you are signed in and Pro features are on, the app saves a backup automatically about once a day, and you can also start one yourself in the Profile tab. The backup contains your trips with their start and end points (not the detailed GPS route), expenses, fuel records, Work Mode settings and your app settings, including any profile, vehicle, insurance and accountant details you entered. It is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and linked to your account.
  • Files you export — PDF and CSV reports and backup files are created on your phone and go only where you save or send them. We never receive them.
  • Website launch list — requests from the form on marvroute.com are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).

Google may store this data on servers in the United States or other countries. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data Retention

  • Trips, fuel logs and expenses stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Your cloud backup is kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete the backup. Each new backup replaces the previous one.
  • Account information is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Launch-list requests are kept only as long as we need them to send the launch announcement, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • When we delete data from our Firebase project, Google may take up to 180 days to clear it from its backup systems.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only:

  • With Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which runs sign-in, cloud backup and the website launch list for us, and with Google Sign-In if you choose to sign in with Google
  • With GitHub, which hosts the marvroute.com website
  • When you choose to, for example when you export a PDF or CSV report and send it to someone
  • When required by law, or to protect someone's safety

Maps in the app are OpenStreetMap map images downloaded from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers (tile.openstreetmap.org), which receive your IP address and the map area on screen, as with any website. Addresses for your trips, and addresses you search for, are looked up with your phone's built-in geocoding service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android), which receives the coordinates or the address text. These services never receive your email address or account ID.

6. Permissions

The app may request:

  • 📍 Location — for trip tracking and automatic trip detection
  • 🔔 Notifications — for trip alerts and maintenance reminders, and for the notice shown while tracking runs in the background
  • 🔋 Battery optimization exemption (Android) — so the system does not stop trip recording in the background

Each permission is optional. Denying one only disables the related feature — the rest of the app keeps working. Saving and sharing files goes through your phone's own save and share screens, so the app does not need a storage permission.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Cloud connections are encrypted in transit, and access to backups is restricted to your own authenticated account. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you without unreasonable delay, as required by law — including Idaho Code § 28-51-105 and, for people in Canada, the rules described in section 9.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete individual trips and expenses in the app
  • Stop automatic cloud backups by signing out of your account or by switching Pro features off — backups are made only while you are signed in with Pro features on
  • Delete your account together with its cloud backup in the app — Profile → Privacy & Data → Delete account — or by email (see Delete account)
  • Ask us by email to delete only your cloud backup and keep your account
  • Stop location tracking at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove the data stored on your device
  • Request a copy of the personal data associated with your account, or ask us to correct it
  • Ask us to remove you from the launch list

Depending on where you live (the USA, Canada, the EU, and others), you may have additional rights to access, correct, port, or erase your personal data. Contact us using the details in section 13 and we will respond within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires.

9. If You Live in Canada

MarV Route is offered to drivers in Canada, so Canadian privacy laws — the federal PIPEDA and, in Québec, the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector — may apply to how we handle your personal information.

  • Person responsible. The owner of MarV Space is accountable for how we handle personal information and is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. Contact details are in section 13.
  • Consent. We handle your information with your consent, which you give when you use the app's features and enter information. You can withdraw it at any time — by switching a feature off, deleting the data or your account, or writing to us — although the related features will then stop working.
  • Location. Trip tracking and automatic trip detection are off until you switch them on yourself, and you can switch them off again at any time.
  • Storage outside Canada. Your account, your cloud backup and your launch-list request are stored by Google in the United States or other countries — outside Canada and Québec. They are subject to the laws of those countries and may be accessible to courts and authorities there.
  • Access and correction. You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and to have it corrected. We answer within 30 days.
  • Security incidents. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada — and, for people in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information — and we keep a record of every breach for as long as the law requires.
  • Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

10. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Do Not Track

MarV Route and this website do not track you across other companies' apps or websites and do not show ads, so they do not change how they work in response to "Do Not Track" signals.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page and in the app or store listing, and the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
